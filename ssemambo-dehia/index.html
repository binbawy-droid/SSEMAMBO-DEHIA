Founder & Principal Architect

Ssemambo Dehia

Ssemambo Dehia is the founder of BINBAWY TECH UG, a Uganda-based software and technology studio. His work focuses on practical software engineering, artificial intelligence, connected tools and workflow automation.

The studio works with businesses, founders, agencies and individuals to plan and build web applications, business systems, AI-assisted workflows and digital products. Remote project enquiries are welcome from Uganda and internationally.

Background & education

Ugandan background and continuing education

Ssemambo Dehia is Ugandan and was born on 24 June 2004. His education includes studies at Kyaddondo Secondary School in Matugga. He is currently a student at the International University of East Africa (IUEA) in Uganda. His university studies are ongoing; no degree or graduation is claimed here.

His professional interests bring together software development, applied artificial intelligence, digital entrepreneurship and practical technology solutions for organisations and individuals.

Professional focus

Building practical digital systems

The work combines software development with applied AI and automation. Projects may include customer-facing websites, internal business tools, connected APIs, AI-assisted processes and e-commerce systems. The aim is to make technology useful, maintainable and aligned with the problem being solved.

For AI and automation projects, the approach is to define the task first, identify the data and services involved, then connect the required tools with clear human oversight where decisions affect customers, money or access. Local-first and cloud-connected approaches can be considered according to the project requirements.

BINBAWY TECH UG is based in Uganda and accepts remote project enquiries. Scope, deliverables, schedule, pricing and ownership terms are agreed in writing before work begins.
