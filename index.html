Written project scope

Each accepted project is defined in a written quote or Statement of Work, including deliverables, milestones, fees and acceptance criteria.

Milestone visibility

Approved projects can show quotes, delivery progress and milestones in the client workspace. Payment timing is stated in the agreed quote.

Client IP terms

Ownership and licensing of custom deliverables are agreed in writing. Transfer of rights is subject to the signed project terms and any outstanding payment.

Privacy and confidentiality

Share only the information needed for an enquiry. A separate mutual NDA can be reviewed and signed before confidential materials are exchanged.

Cross-border coordination

Based in Uganda (EAT, UTC+3). Remote meetings across GMT, North American and Asian time zones can be arranged by agreement.

Payment transparency

No payment is taken when you submit an enquiry. Accepted payment methods, currency, deposit and refund terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.

BINBAWY TECH UG does not currently advertise third-party escrow, guaranteed two-hour response times or 24/7 human support. Do not send passwords, API secrets or sensitive personal data through public enquiry forms.
