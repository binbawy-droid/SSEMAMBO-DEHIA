A community initiative in development

Make computer skills accessible in Kampala.

We are developing a practical, partner-led initiative to help young people and underserved community members gain confidence using computers, access learning resources and progress into digital careers.

Support the initiativeBecome a partner
Pilot concept · Kampala

Learn by using technology.

The first phase will be shaped with established community organisations, schools, training centres and equipment partners. We are seeking a host venue and delivery partners before announcing class dates or learner intake.

01 · FOUNDATIONS

Everyday computer literacy

Computer basics, typing, file management, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, email and safe internet use.

02 · DIGITAL CONFIDENCE

Work and learning online

Online research, CV preparation, digital communication, responsible use of AI tools and identifying online scams.

03 · NEXT STEPS

Pathways into technology

Introductory coding, web development, problem-solving, creative tools and ethical cybersecurity awareness.

What we need to start

Working devices

Refurbished laptops or desktops, monitors, keyboards, mice, chargers, storage and basic networking equipment.

People and space

Volunteer instructors, mentors, a safe accessible learning venue, power and internet access.

Programme support

Training materials, device refurbishment, transport, connectivity and learner support.

Ways to support

Foundations, companies, suppliers, institutions and individuals can help in practical ways. We welcome in-kind equipment, refurbishment, training time, venue access, connectivity sponsorship and introductions to grant programmes.

Donate equipment

Offer working or repairable laptops, desktops, monitors, accessories or spare parts. We will coordinate inventory and handover with a suitable host partner.

Sponsor a learning cycle

Support a defined pilot with transparent costs for devices, connectivity, learning materials, facilitator time and learner access.

Volunteer or mentor

Contribute practical instruction, career guidance, technical support or mentorship for beginner learners.

Work with us

We are looking for organisations that already serve communities in Kampala and can advise on learner selection, safeguarding, venue access, programme governance and equipment stewardship.

Community and education partners

Schools, youth organisations, community centres, NGOs and established training providers.

Technology suppliers

Computer retailers, distributors, repair centres and corporate IT teams able to provide refurbished stock, discounts, servicing or surplus equipment.

Funders and foundations

CSR teams, foundations, development partners and grant programmes interested in digital inclusion and measurable skills outcomes.

Start a partnership conversation

Tell us what your organisation could contribute or what support your community needs. We will follow up to discuss eligibility, safeguards, responsibilities and a practical pilot plan.
