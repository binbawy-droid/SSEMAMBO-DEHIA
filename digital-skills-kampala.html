Equipment and data protection. Donors should not hand over devices containing accessible business or personal data. Before collection, we will agree the data-sanitisation method and responsibility with the donor. Devices will be inventoried; storage media will be isolated until sanitised and checked. Written wipe confirmation can be provided only where the actual process and verification record support it. We do not currently claim independent certification. Equipment that cannot be safely refurbished will be directed to an appropriate e-waste handler rather than represented as reusable.

How donated equipment will be handled

  1. Agree ownership, collection, device condition and data-handling responsibilities with the donor.
  2. Record make, model, serial number where available, accessories and visible condition at handover.
  3. Keep storage media protected until an agreed sanitisation process is completed and recorded.
  4. Assess, repair and test suitable equipment before assigning it to learning use.
  5. Track equipment placement and share appropriate progress updates with donors.
  6. Route irreparable or unsafe equipment to a suitable e-waste service.

Governance and legal readiness

Registration roadmap for Kampala

The right route depends on where the initiative will operate, who will govern it and which institutions it intends to approach. Registration does not guarantee grants, tax exemption, a bank account or acceptance of an MoU. Confirm the responsible office, forms and fees before paying or filing.

Route A · Community-Based Organisation (CBO)

Potentially suitable for a community-level pilot whose work is limited to a defined local area. Under the NGO Bureau's published guidance, CBO applications are considered by the District NGO Monitoring Committee, following a recommendation from the Sub-county NGO Monitoring Committee. The application is submitted to the District Community Development Officer's office; do not assume an LC1 letter alone replaces the required committee recommendation.

  1. Ask KCCA Rubaga Division's Community Development Office which office currently receives Kampala CBO applications and which Sub-county NGO Monitoring Committee covers the proposed operating area.
  2. Prepare a constitution, founding meeting minutes and attendance list, leadership details, a practical work plan and budget, and the proposed geographical area of operation.
  3. Obtain the required Sub-county NGO Monitoring Committee recommendation and complete NGO Bureau Form K.
  4. Submit the hard-copy application in the required order to the responsible District Community Development Officer's office, with proof of payment.
  5. Wait for the District NGO Monitoring Committee decision and collect the certificate and permit only after approval.

Published NGO Bureau fees: UGX 40,000 certificate of registration and UGX 60,000 permit of operation. Confirm current fees and payment instructions directly before filing. The permit must be renewed; annual returns and reporting obligations also apply.

Official CBO requirements, submission and fees · Official Form K and related forms

Route B · Company limited by guarantee

Potentially suitable where the founders want a separate incorporated legal entity with a non-profit purpose and no share capital. It is a company form, not by itself an NGO Bureau registration, grant approval or tax-exempt status.

  1. Agree the organisation's purpose, members, directors, guarantee commitments, governance and asset-lock/dissolution provisions.
  2. Check and reserve the proposed name through URSB's Online Business Registration System.
  3. Prepare the memorandum and articles of association and submit the required incorporation details for a company limited by guarantee without share capital.
  4. After incorporation, obtain tax guidance from URA and ask the bank about its account-opening and signatory requirements.
  5. If the entity will operate as an NGO, separately check the NGO Bureau's current registration and permit requirements, including recommendations, governance, work plans and reporting.

Published URSB fees: UGX 35,000 for company name reservation and UGX 105,000 for registration of a company without nominal share capital. Other filing, professional, tax and compliance costs may apply; verify the current schedule.

URSB official website · URSB fee schedule · URA
Important before choosing. A CBO route and a company-limited-by-guarantee route have different legal consequences and governance costs. Neither automatically qualifies BINBAWY TECH UG for Mastercard Foundation, Google.org, UNDP, KCCA or other institutional funding. Some opportunities require NGO Bureau registration, audited accounts, a track record, safeguarding and financial controls, or delivery through an eligible registered partner. Seek written guidance from KCCA/NGO Bureau and independent Ugandan legal or accounting advice before committing funds.
